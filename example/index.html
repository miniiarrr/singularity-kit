the page keeps scrolling

The black hole scrolls away with the hero and stops drawing once it is out of view. Move the pointer through the gas: it parts and closes again behind it. Come close to the surfer and he carves away.

Add ?at=16.5 to the URL to freeze the final frame, ?at=4.8 for the explosion, ?from=10 to skip the intro. With reduced motion on, the final frame is shown still.

Three modes: full (the whole story), galaxy (the galaxy alone, turning), hole (the black hole from its birth, with the surfer). A page picks one with data-singularity-mode on the anchor or mount({ mode }); ?mode= overrides it here.
